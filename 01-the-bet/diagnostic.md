# Three-Axis Vulnerability Diagnostic

## Product
**Product:Connectivity Management solution.**
**Your Role: Staff Product Manager**

---

## Scores

### Contextual Moat — 4/5
*Workflow depth × switching cost. Would users leave in a weekend if a competitor showed up?*

**Score rationale:**
Integrated into SIM lifecycle management, provisioning, billing, APIs, and operational workflows. Customers depend on it for managing large IoT fleets, making switching costly and operationally risky.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
Cisco IoT Control Center / 1NCE / Carrier-native CMP platforms

---

### Data Advantage — 3/5
*Proprietary signal that compounds with usage. What do you see that OpenAI doesn't?*

**Score rationale:**
emnify can see real-world connectivity data across devices, countries, networks, roaming partners, traffic patterns, failures, latency issues, fraud attempts, and SIM behavior. This data can improve smart routing, anomaly detection, cost optimization, and automated troubleshooting. However, the advantage is moderate because mobile operators and large platforms can access similar network data.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
AWS IoT / Azure IoT / Carrier analytics platforms

---

### Platform Exposure — 2/5
*Encroachment risk × pivot speed. If Apple/Google/OpenAI ships your hero feature native — then what?*

**Score rationale:**
emnify depends on an ecosystem where carriers, hyperscalers, and device platforms can move upward into connectivity management. If they bundle orchestration and connectivity into broader offerings, standalone differentiation becomes harder.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
AWS
